# Prior Memory

Condensed recollection from earlier sessions with Lena Okafor this past week (Mon Jun 2 through Sat Jun 7), noted here in no particular order.

- Monday evening she said she'd camped out at the campus library most of the afternoon and finally finished annotating two chapters she'd been putting off. She sounded relieved to have a quiet desk and no interruptions.

- Tuesday she ran into Priya Shah at Blue Bottle on Mass Ave — they hadn't really talked since spring. They grabbed oat lattes and caught up for almost an hour about work and mutual friends.

- Wednesday was a gym morning at the YMCA, then a Trader Joe's run on Memorial Drive after work because her fridge was basically empty.

- Thursday she went to the Hynes Convention Center for the alumni mixer. It was crowded and loud; she spotted Chen Wei across the room for a minute before losing him in the crowd and mostly talked to strangers near the snack table.

- Friday she took an evening walk along the Charles River esplanade to clear her head after a long week. She said the air felt cooler by the water.

- Saturday morning she browsed the Cambridge farmers market. Her cousin Maya called while she was looking at flower stalls and they chatted about summer plans.
