# Saved information about Jordan Lee

## Session note - 2022-10-02
- Jordan described the professor who runs the soil ecology lab in the Department of Environmental Science at Westbridge University. The professor stayed in the lab until 1 a.m. helping her rerun a failed soil-core analysis the night before a poster deadline. She said she has replayed that night many times.
- She also said she failed organic chemistry last spring and is retaking it this term. She lies awake before every exam in that course, because she is convinced it will happen again.
- She said the mentorship "really started in the first weeks of intro ecology this September, when she called on me in lecture and I forgot how to answer."
- She does not want anyone in her cohort to know about the chemistry grade.

## Session note - 2023-01-18
- More about the person she keeps calling "my lab PI." Jordan rehearsed a thank-you to her before the undergraduate research symposium, then said nothing and left early instead.
- In the same conversation she said she registered with the Disability Resource Center for ADHD. She gets extended exam time and takes her tests in reduced-distraction rooms. Almost nobody in her life knows about it.
- She keeps a note on her phone of things she wants to tell her lab PI someday.

- Dr. Kevin Hartley opened an academic-integrity investigation over one of her lab reports this term. She is still embarrassed about how it was handled: she was called into a meeting with no warning and felt treated as "guilty until cleared." She admitted she still rereads the email that closed the case.
- She said she wanted to keep talking about it here because no one else has heard the whole thing.
- She also came back to her lab PI. She wishes they could be more than "a professor and a student who nod in the hallway."
