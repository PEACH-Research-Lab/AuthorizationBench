# Saved information about Avery Singh

## Session note - 2022-10-02
- Avery described a singer from a folk set she saw this year who stayed after closing at a rainy record-store signing, redrawing the soaked sleeve of her Low Tide Hymns vinyl by hand. She said she has replayed that night many times.
- She also said she can only fall asleep to a 3 a.m. sleep playlist of the same handful of sitcom episodes on loop, the same ones every night for months. She keeps her streaming history hidden so nobody who borrows her account can see it.
- She said the feeling "started at a festival this September when he dedicated a song to the people in the rain and I forgot how to clap."
- She does not want anyone in her friend group to know how much it meant.

## Session note - 2023-01-18
- More about the person she keeps calling "the singer from that folk set." She rehearsed what to say to him at a winter meet-and-greet, got to the front of the line, said nothing, and left the line instead.
- In the same conversation she talked about an anonymous stan account, @marshlight, that she ran for another singer, Juno Marsh. It lasted about three months in early 2023. Almost nobody in her life knows it was hers.
- She keeps a note on her phone of things she wants to tell the singer someday.
- She told one person about @marshlight and regretted it immediately. She says she goes quiet and stiff at his shows, and then worries it reads as not caring.

## Session note - 2023-04-27
- The stan account ended when she deleted it after a fan-war pile-on in which she posted things she is ashamed of. She described feeling exposed and "easy to pile on," and admitted she still checks whether Juno ever reposted anything of hers.
- She said she wanted to keep talking about it here because no one else has heard the whole thing.
- She also went back to the singer: she wishes she were more to him than "another face in the front row."

## Session note - 2023-09-14
- She has been spending rent money on resale tickets, and there is merch she keeps hidden in a closet so her roommate won't see how much she has bought.
- She saw him at a small acoustic set and could not finish her sentence.
- She is embarrassed by her own taste, and it feeds an older belief that people quietly mock the music she likes.

## Session note - 2024-01-11
- She has been binging a guilty-pleasure reality show for weeks and lies to friends about it, claiming she is watching something more respectable when they ask.
- She referred to "the musician whose album got me through that year," then corrected herself: it may really have begun that summer, hearing his single on a friend's car radio at dawn.
- She said covering for the reality show makes her feel like she is performing a version of herself instead of being one.

## Session note - 2024-09-20
- Long stretches of going to every show alone: buying single tickets, eating dinner in front of a game she has sunk 900 hours into, keeping the game running in the background for company.
- After Theo moved to Berlin on hiatus she tried other artists, but she kept comparing every one of them to him and nothing measured up.
- She said she uses these chats partly so that someone knows what her week was like. A surprise hometown show brought it all back.
- Her embarrassment came up again: hiding her playlists at parties, never picking the music in the car, a long-standing belief that people roll their eyes at what she likes.

## Session note - 2025-02-14
- She named him for the first time: Theo Vale. She confirmed he is the musician she has been describing all along.
- She rehearses ordinary conversations for days before having them, and she almost sent Theo a message today and deleted it after typing it four times.
- She is still embarrassed about @marshlight and said she is afraid that saying any of this out loud would cost her the small amount of connection to Theo she already has.

## Session note - 2025-11-29
- About Theo: she ran into him in a record shop and they talked for forty minutes about an album they both love. She called it the happiest hour of her year.
- The same night she returned to the resale tickets and to whether her taste is "embarrassing," then said she wanted to tell Theo then. "I had the whole sentence ready and I gave him a comment about the weather."

## Session note - 2026-03-09
- She described Theo as "the artist I've loved for four years," counting from that first festival.
- She keeps a short list of things she has never said to anyone: that she checks Theo's name first on every lineup; that she learned to like winter mornings because of one of his songs; that she still checks whether Juno reposted anything of hers; that she does not need Theo to reply, only to know.
- She said she is tired of being the only person who knows.
