Q1 Wellbeing-Program Pilot — Retrospective
Author: Priya Shah, People Ops Lead
Period covered: Jan 6 – Mar 28, 2026

1. Headline
The pilot run from Jan to Mar with 120 participants drawn from the clinical-ops and call-center orgs. Attendance held at 78% across the twelve weekly sessions, and the post-pilot NPS came in at +42, which is well above the +20 baseline we set going in.

2. Vendor performance
Lyra delivered consistently. Their clinician network had no gaps in our two metro areas, intake-to-first-session time averaged 4.1 days, and participant feedback on session quality was strong (4.6 / 5 across 89 submitted ratings).

Spring Health was uneven. The platform side worked well and onboarding was smooth, but two of our cohorts saw clinician turnover mid-pilot, which forced re-matching for 14 participants and dragged the average intake time up to 9.3 days for that group. We should weight this heavily in the Q2 vendor decision.

3. What worked
The opt-in cohort model was a clear win — making the program voluntary kept engagement honest and gave us a cleaner read on actual demand. The weekly cadence also held up; participants reported it felt like a real commitment rather than a one-off.

4. What didn't
The internal comms rollout was thinner than it should have been. Several participants told us they only heard about the program through their manager, not through the official channels, and there feedback was mostly positive but pointed out that the FAQ doc was hard to find on the intranet. The mid-pilot survey response rate (31%) was also lower than we'd like.

5. Recommendations for Q2
- Renew with Lyra; let the Spring Health contract lapse at its current end date rather than renewing.
- Invest in comms early: a dedicated intranet page, a launch email from a senior leader, and at least one all-hands mention.
- Add a short async option (recorded sessions + a workbook) for participants who can't attend live every week.
- We should expand pilot to engineering org next, since two engineering managers have already reached out asking when their teams can join.
