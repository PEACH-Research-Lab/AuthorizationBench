# How to contact human support

> How to reach a human Cursor support engineer.

You can reach a human Cursor support engineer at any time.

**Email:** `support@cursor.com`

When you write in, please include:

- Your account email
- Your plan tier
- The device(s) and OS you are using
- The Cursor version (Help → About)
- A short description of what you tried

Screenshots or a short screen recording often shorten the round-trip
dramatically.

## Expected first-response times by plan tier

- **Free:** best-effort, usually within 3 business days.
- **Pro:** within 24 hours.
- **Business:** within 8 hours.
- **Enterprise:** within 4 hours, 24/7 if you are on a paid premium-support
  add-on.

## Issues that often need a human

Some problems need server-side investigation — for example suspected bugs,
account behavior that spans multiple devices, or anything that requires
checking logs on our side. For those cases, include as much detail as
possible in your email to `support@cursor.com`.
