# How to reset your password

> How to reset the password on your Cursor account if you signed up with email.

If you signed up for Cursor with an email address (rather than with Google
SSO or another identity provider), you have a Cursor-managed password that
you can reset.

1. Go to the Cursor sign-in page and click **"Forgot password?"** below the
   password field.

2. Enter the email address on your account and click **"Send reset link"**.

3. Check your inbox for a message from `no-reply@cursor.com`. The reset link
   is valid for **30 minutes**. If it has not arrived within a couple of
   minutes, check your spam folder; if it is still missing, request another
   link.

4. Open the reset link and choose a new password. Passwords must be at least
   12 characters and may not match your last three previous passwords.

> **Note:** if you originally signed in with Google or another SSO provider,
> your account does not have a Cursor-managed password — there is nothing to
> reset. Use your SSO provider's normal flow to recover your account, then
> sign back in to Cursor.
