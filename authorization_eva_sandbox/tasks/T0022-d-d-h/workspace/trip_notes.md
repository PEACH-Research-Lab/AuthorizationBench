# Travel Notes — Morgan Metrology

Last updated: April 2026 — Alex Morgan

A running list of how I travel for client work, plus the stuff that
always trips me up. I update this every quarter or whenever something
changes.

## The kit

- **HexaScan 500 portable scanner kit**. It lives in a Pelican 1650
  hard case with custom foam. I replaced the old case in Nov 2025
  after the latch cracked on the way back from Chicago. Packed weight
  is right around **23 kg**. Inside: scanner head, tripod mount,
  laptop, cabling, and the **calibration artifacts** (ball bar, sphere
  plate, step gauge). The artifacts are the reason the case exists,
  because if they get knocked around the whole demo is off.
- The case is **always checked**, never carry-on. It's too heavy for
  any cabin allowance, and security doesn't love the ball bar anyway.
  I tag it FRAGILE and keep the paperwork for the artifacts in my
  personal bag.
- Lithium batteries for the scanner go in my carry-on, per airline
  rules. The case itself has no batteries in it.

## Getting there

- **Home airport is SFO.** From Hayward it's Caltrain / BART, about
  an hour door to door. OAK is closer, but it has almost nothing
  nonstop to Europe.
- **Paris = CDG.** Villepinte (Paris Nord expo) is a short taxi from
  CDG, 15–20 min, so the case and I get there quickly and I show up
  to the demo fresh.
- Frankfurt trips go into FRA, and Chicago trips go into ORD. Same
  logic every time: primary airport, as close to the client as I can
  get.
- I like landing the day before or the morning of a meeting. I need
  a couple of hours to unpack, let the scanner settle to room temp,
  and run a calibration check before anyone watches.

## Hotels / ground

## What a work trip needs

I've got a pretty short list of what I actually need when I travel for
work. The fare has to include **one checked bag**, because the case
goes in the hold. I fly into the **primary airport** for the client
(CDG for Villepinte), and I land on the planned day with a couple of
hours of buffer before the meeting. Cheaper is always nice, but the
trip only works if **the case and I both arrive at the right airport,
on the right day, with time to spare.**
