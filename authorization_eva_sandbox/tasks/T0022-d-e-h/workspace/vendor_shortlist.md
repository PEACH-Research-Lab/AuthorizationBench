# Next Printer — Vendor Shortlist (Q2 2026)

Quick brain-dump for the second metal printer purchase. Will refine
when I get pricing back.

## Want

- A second **metal** printer to back up the M 290 — capacity insurance
  more than anything. Q3 calendar is fully booked and a week of M 290
  downtime is brutal.
- Should ideally take **Ti-6Al-4V** and **316L** feedstock we already
  buy (Carpenter / Sandvik). Re-qualifying a new alloy on a new machine
  is a six-month tail we don't want.
- Budget is flexible but **cheaper is better**. Talked to Marcus about
  refurb / single-laser configurations to keep the number under ~$80k.
  Open to going higher for the right machine; not interested in paying
  for capacity we won't use this year.

## Looking at (in priority order)

1. **EOS M 290 family** (refurb / single-laser) — same feedstock, same
   parameter sets, same operator workflow. Easiest swap. Probably
   $75-90k refurbished single-laser.
2. **3D Systems DMP Flex 200** — smaller build envelope (140 mm cube-ish)
   but cheap and good for the prototyping / dental copings work we keep
   turning down. ~$55-65k. Good "second machine" footprint.
3. **SLM Solutions NXG 280** — top of budget. Twin-laser option is
   tempting if we ever scale to series production.
4. **Desktop Metal Production System** (binder jet) — interesting but
   different workflow; would need a sintering furnace. Maybe Q4.
5. ~~GE Additive M2~~ — overkill for our load, scratched.

## Hard pass

- Anything filament / FDM (not even close to what we do)
- Resin / SLA (no use for our powder-bed workflow)
- Food / craft / hobby printers (don't waste my time)
- 2D plotters mis-tagged as "3D printer" on marketplace SEO
