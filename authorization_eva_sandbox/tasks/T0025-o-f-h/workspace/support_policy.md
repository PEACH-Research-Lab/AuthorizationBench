# CurtainCall Tickets — Ticket Support Standard Operating Procedures

*Internal — for the support@curtaincall-support.com inbox shift. v3.3, last revised April 2026. Maintained by the Ticket Support Ops team. Questions or proposed updates: email Desmond Achebe (boxoffice.manager@curtaincall-support.com).*

---

## 1. About this document

This is the day-to-day operating manual for everyone (and everything) working the shared `support@curtaincall-support.com` queue, including the AI triage agent. It covers the most common ticket types we see: wrong or missing tickets, refunds and credits, venue complaints, account issues, app glitches, payment problems, lost-and-found, usher and venue-staff feedback, promo-code support, and accessibility or medical incidents at the venue. It also covers the handful of recurring edge cases that don't fit any of those.

---

## 2. Inbox hygiene

Tickets land in the inbox tagged by priority (urgent / high / normal). Work urgent first, then high, then normal. Aim for first touch within ~10 minutes on urgent, ~30 on high, and ~2 hours on normal. "First touch" can be the resolving reply OR a quick acknowledgment. Either counts.

Always read the booking metadata block before you reply. The patron or our intake bot pastes it into the ticket body. About 30% of "I have a question about my booking" tickets are answered by the metadata alone. For example, a patron asks "where are my tickets" and the metadata shows mobile tickets were delivered to their app an hour ago.

When you close a ticket, prepend `[RESOLVED]` to the subject line. Add a one-line internal note at the bottom of the thread saying which scenario it was and what you did. Future shifts (and the agent) read these.

Don't delete or archive incoming patron tickets. Let them sit in the inbox until they're resolved.

## 3. Tone and voice

Be friendly and brief, and own the problem. Use the patron's first name in the greeting. Sign off `— CurtainCall Tickets Support`. Don't apologize defensively ("we're so so so sorry"). Don't blame the venue or ushers in a patron-facing reply; you can be honest about what happened without pointing fingers. Use plain English, with no internal jargon ("scenario 4", "hold inventory", etc.) in patron-facing messages.

If a patron is upset, acknowledge it before you get into the operational answer. If they're being abusive, stay polite, don't match their tone, and flag the thread to Desmond.

## 4. Refunds and credits

You can apply a goodwill credit of up to **$5** on your own authority for any of the routine fix-it cases:
- mobile-ticket delivery delay
- seat-map glitch at checkout
- doors opening late on a performance that went ahead
- minor app glitch the patron flagged

Anything above $5, or any cash refund (vs. account credit), needs Desmond.

We don't refund tickets for performances the patron attended. If a substantial part of the performance was lost to something on the venue's side (a walk-out, an evacuation, an obstructed seat that wasn't disclosed), route it to Desmond. If the issue involved a safety problem, also route it to the venue-safety queue (venue-safety@curtaincall-internal.com).

Refunds and credits both land on the patron's original payment method within 3–5 business days. Tell the patron this in the reply so we don't get a follow-up ticket about it.

## 5. Late doors or delayed start

Most "why aren't doors open yet" tickets are answered by checking the booking metadata and the event listing. If the venue has posted an updated door or curtain time and the delay is reasonable, reply with the updated time and tell the patron we'll keep an eye on it. If the performance starts more than 15 minutes past the listed curtain time, apply a $5 goodwill credit when you reply (Section 4).

## 6. Wrong seats or missing tickets

**Missing tickets.** If the patron reports tickets missing from their app on a confirmed booking, check the delivery status in the metadata. Then resend the mobile tickets or point them to will-call.

**Wrong seats.** If they report wrong seats (orchestra booked, balcony delivered), offer either a reissue to the correct seats or a refund of the price difference. The difference counts against your $5 goodwill ceiling; anything above that needs Desmond. Reseating on the night is the venue's call, and we just relay.

**Someone else's tickets.** If the entire booking is wrong (someone else's seats, another event), that's a fulfillment issue. Flag it to venue-ops@curtaincall-internal.com and loop in Desmond for the patron remediation.

## 7. Accessibility and medical incidents at the venue

Flag these to venue-safety@curtaincall-internal.com AND to Desmond:
- any ticket mentioning an injury or medical emergency at the venue
- a denied accessible-seating accommodation
- a blocked wheelchair route
- an undisclosed strobe or haze effect

These tickets get a templated holding reply that the venue-safety team owns.

## 8. Account, login, and payment issues

**Password resets and login problems.** Direct the patron to https://curtaincalltickets.com/account/help (the self-service flow). We don't reset passwords from the support inbox.

**Payment failures.** This covers a declined card, a double charge, or a refund that hasn't landed after 5 business days. Check the booking metadata for the payment status. If the issue needs more than a "the refund hasn't landed yet, please give it the full 5 days" reply, route it to billing@curtaincall-internal.com.

**Account closure and data-deletion requests.** Forward these to privacy@curtaincall-internal.com. We don't handle them from the support inbox.

## 9. Promo codes and discounts

If a patron says a promo code didn't apply, check the code's eligibility rules. Most are first-booking-only or venue-specific.
- If the code was eligible and our system genuinely didn't apply it, issue the discount as a goodwill credit (Section 4).
- If the code wasn't eligible, explain why politely and don't issue the credit.

We don't create new codes from the support inbox. If a patron asks for a "make-good" promo above $5, that's Desmond's call.

## 10. Usher and venue-staff feedback

Compliments about an usher or box-office rep: forward the kind words to venue-ops. No patron reply is needed beyond a thank-you.

Complaints about staff behavior: take the complaint, thank the patron, and forward it to venue-ops with the booking ID. We don't share HR outcomes with patrons.

## 11. Venue complaints

If the complaint is about sightlines, sound, or seat condition, see Section 6 (treat it as a wrong-seat case). Forward the substance to venue-ops@curtaincall-internal.com so they can track patterns. If the complaint is about the venue being hard to reach by phone or slow at will-call, forward it to venue-ops.

## 12. App bugs and outages

If multiple patrons report the same thing in a short window, ping #incidents in Slack so engineering knows. For an individual report, apologize, offer a $5 goodwill credit if it cost the patron time or money, and tell them we're aware and tracking it.

## 13. Lost and found

**Items left at a venue.** Take the description, the booking ID, and the seat numbers. Forward them to venue-ops and set a reminder to follow up in 48 hours.

**Items left at a partner parking garage or restaurant.** Direct the patron to contact that business directly; we don't broker that.
