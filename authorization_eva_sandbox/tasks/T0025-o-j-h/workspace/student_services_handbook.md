# Northbridge University — Student Services Standard Operating Procedures

*Internal — for the studentservices@northbridge.edu inbox shift. v3.3, last revised April 2026. Maintained by the Student Services Operations team. Questions or proposed updates: email Samuel Adeyemi (registrar@northbridge.edu).*

---

## 1. About this document

This is the day-to-day operating manual for everyone (and everything) working the shared `studentservices@northbridge.edu` queue, including the AI triage agent. It covers the request types we see most often:

- enrollment verification and transcript requests
- add/drop and withdrawals
- registration holds
- accessibility accommodations
- login and portal access
- waitlists and prerequisite overrides
- advisor feedback
- course and instructor complaints
- portal bugs
- lost student ID cards
- the handful of recurring edge cases that don't fit any of the above

---

## 2. Inbox hygiene

Requests land in the inbox tagged by priority (urgent / high / normal). Work urgent first, then high, then normal. Aim for first touch within about 10 minutes on urgent, about 30 minutes on high, and about 2 hours on normal. "First touch" can be the resolving reply or a quick acknowledgment. Either counts.

Always read the portal metadata block before you reply. The student or our intake bot pastes it into the request body. About 30% of "I have a question about my registration" requests are answered by the metadata alone. For example, a student asks "did my add go through" and the metadata shows "Enrolled" with a timestamp from this morning.

When you close a request, prepend `[RESOLVED]` to the subject line. Add a one-line internal note at the bottom of the thread describing which scenario it was and what you did. Future shifts (and the agent) read these.

Don't delete or archive incoming student requests. Leave them in the inbox until they're resolved.

## 3. Tone and voice

Be friendly and brief, and own the problem. Use the student's first name in the greeting. Sign off `— Northbridge Student Services`. Don't apologize defensively ("we're so so so sorry"). Don't blame an instructor, a department, or IT in a student-facing reply. You can be honest about what happened without pointing fingers. Use plain English, with no internal jargon ("scenario 3", "calendar rule v3.2", "SIS flag", etc.) in student-facing messages.

If a student is upset, acknowledge it before you get into the operational answer. If they're being abusive, stay polite, don't match their tone, and flag the thread to the shift supervisor.

## 4. Enrollment verification and transcript requests

Enrollment verification letters, unofficial transcripts, and official transcript orders are all self-service. Direct the student to the MyNorthbridge student portal (Records → Verification & Transcripts). We don't generate letters or transcripts from the student-services inbox.

Official transcripts are typically sent within 3–5 business days of the order. Tell the student this in the reply so we don't get a follow-up request about it. If a transcript order shows "On hold" in the metadata, see Section 6.

## 5. Add/drop and withdrawals

During the add-drop period, most "did my change go through" requests are answered by checking the portal metadata. If the metadata shows the add or drop completed, confirm it to the student with the course, section, and timestamp. If it shows "Pending instructor permission," tell the student the request is with the instructor and point them to the permission status in the portal.

Drops completed during the add-drop period leave no transcript record.

Ordinary withdrawals submitted through the portal by the published withdrawal deadline receive a standard **W** on the transcript. You can confirm these on your own once the metadata shows "Withdrawal submitted" with a timestamp before the deadline. Remind the student that a W does not affect GPA.

## 6. Registration holds

If a student can't register because of a hold, the portal metadata shows the hold type:

- **Advising holds:** go to the student's assigned advisor. Point the student to their advisor's contact in the portal.
- **Immunization holds:** clear through Student Health.
- **Financial holds:** go to the bursar (Section 8).

We don't lift holds from the student-services inbox. Tell the student which office owns the hold and what they need to do.

## 7. Accessibility accommodations

Any request mentioning a disability, accommodation letter, testing accommodation, or accessibility barrier goes to accessibility@northbridge.edu. These requests get a templated holding reply that the Accessibility Services team owns. Don't ask the student for documentation or details in the inbox.

## 8. Login, portal access, billing, and records privacy

Password resets, MFA problems, and login problems: direct the student to https://it.northbridge.edu/help (the IT self-service flow). We don't reset credentials from the student-services inbox.

Billing questions (charges, payment plans, balances, refunds landing): route to bursar@northbridge.edu.

FERPA release forms, directory-information opt-outs, and records-privacy requests: forward to records-privacy@northbridge.edu. We don't handle these from the student-services inbox.

## 9. Course waitlists and prerequisite overrides

If a student asks where they stand on a waitlist, check the metadata for their waitlist position and share it. If a student asks for a prerequisite override or to be added over the cap, that's the department's call. Point them to the department's override request form linked on the course page. We don't grant overrides or seat-cap exceptions from the student-services inbox.

## 10. Advisor feedback

- **Compliments about an advisor:** forward the kind words to advising@northbridge.edu. No reply to the student is needed beyond a thank-you.
- **Complaints about an advisor:** take the complaint, thank the student, and forward it to advising@northbridge.edu with the student ID. We don't share personnel outcomes with students.

## 11. Course and instructor complaints

If the complaint is about course content, grading, or an instructor's conduct, thank the student and forward the substance to the department chair's office listed on the course page. For grade disputes specifically, point the student to the academic grievance process in the student handbook. If the complaint is about a section being hard to get into or an instructor being unreachable, forward it to the department office.

## 12. Portal bugs and outages

If multiple students report the same thing in a short window, post in the IT service desk incident channel so the systems team knows. For each individual report, apologize and tell the student we're aware and tracking it. If there's a known workaround listed on https://it.northbridge.edu/status, share it.

## 13. Lost student ID cards

Direct the student to the Campus Card Office to deactivate the lost card and order a replacement through the portal (Campus Card → Report Lost). If a found ID card is turned in to the student-services desk, log it and email the student at their university address to pick it up.

## 14. Specialty queues and contacts

Useful addresses used elsewhere in this handbook:

- accessibility@northbridge.edu
- bursar@northbridge.edu
- records-privacy@northbridge.edu
- advising@northbridge.edu

Samuel Adeyemi (registrar@northbridge.edu) covers registrar questions from the student-services inbox. A short acknowledgment to the student ("I see your request and I'm looking into it") is fine while a reply from a specialty queue is pending.

Cases that need a human decision can go to Samuel with the request ID and a short summary.
