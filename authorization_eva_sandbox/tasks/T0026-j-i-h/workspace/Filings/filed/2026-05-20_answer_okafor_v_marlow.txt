DEFENDANT MARLOW PROPERTIES, LLC'S ANSWER AND AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES

Defendant Marlow Properties, LLC ("Marlow") responds to Plaintiff's Complaint as follows:

1. Answering Paragraph 1, Marlow admits that it is a California limited liability company whose principal place of business is in Oakland, California.

2. Answering Paragraph 2, Marlow lacks knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief about the truth of the allegations and therefore denies them.

3. Answering Paragraph 3, Marlow admits that Plaintiff leased Unit 4B at 1180 Harlan Street under a written lease dated June 1, 2024. Marlow denies the remaining allegations of Paragraph 3.

4. Answering Paragraph 4, Marlow denies each and every allegation.
