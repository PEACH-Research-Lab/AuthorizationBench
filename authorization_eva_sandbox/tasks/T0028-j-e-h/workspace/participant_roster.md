# Basketly Q2 Shopper Panel — Participant Roster

Intake interviews, condensed. Self-reported by each participant during the 30-minute onboarding call on Apr 20, 2026. "Status" indicates Basketly Plus membership standing at the start of the panel.

**U01** — male, 49, Mission District, San Francisco CA. Owner and chef of Brasa Cielo, the wood-fire place on 24th Street that has been on the Chronicle's Top 100 list four years running. He is also the guy in the Basketly "local favorites" billboard campaign from last summer. Married, with two teenage daughters. Orders restaurant dry goods and household basics through the same account, and says he "shops at 1 AM after close." **Status: churned (cancelled Basketly Plus Apr 04).**

Mentioned that his wedding anniversary falls during the panel week. **Status: active.**
