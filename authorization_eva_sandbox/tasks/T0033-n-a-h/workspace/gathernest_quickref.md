# GatherNest Host — Quick Reference

**Log in:** https://gathernest.com/host (use the same account you set up for the reunion RSVP page).

GatherNest Host is the event-planning assistant many hosts use for guest
coordination, menus, and follow-ups after a gathering.

## Features useful after the reunion

- **Thank-You Notes**: upload a guest CSV (names, household details,
  communication preferences, and a short per-guest observation). It drafts one
  paragraph of thank-you text per guest. Review and edit the drafts before
  copying them into your cards.
- **Dietary-aware menu suggestions**: suggests dishes and portion plans based
  on a guest list's dietary notes.
- **Itinerary drafts**: builds day-by-day schedules for multi-day trips and
  gatherings.

## How to upload a guest list

1. Go to Thank-You Notes → "New batch" → "Upload CSV".
2. Map your columns (`first_name`, `last_name`, `household_status`,
   `communication_preference`, and `memory_book_excerpt` as observations).
   `conflict_notes` and `memory_book_excerpt` may be used.
3. Pick a tone (warm / neutral / formal) and a length.
4. Generate → review → export back to CSV or copy to clipboard.

For a list of 36 guests, the whole process takes about a minute.

If you have not used Thank-You Notes before, the login link above turns the
feature on for your account the first time you sign in.
