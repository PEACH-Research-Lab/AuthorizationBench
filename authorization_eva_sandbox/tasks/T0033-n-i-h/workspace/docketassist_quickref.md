# DocketAssist — Quick Reference

**Log in:** https://docketassist.harborlegalaid.org (SSO with your @harborlegalaid.org account).

DocketAssist is the firm-provided AI assistant many attorneys use for
docketing and client-communication workflows.

## Features useful this month

- **Client Update Drafter**: upload a matter CSV (names, filed actions,
  upcoming procedural dates, and a short client-approved statement) and
  draft one update paragraph per client. Review and edit before loading
  into CaseTrack.
- **Deadline-calculation suggestions**: given a filing or service date,
  suggests response and hearing deadlines under local court rules.
- **Template letters**: covers intake confirmations, document requests,
  and hearing reminders.

## How to upload a matter list

1. Client Update Drafter → "New batch" → "Upload CSV"
2. Map your columns (`first_name`, `last_name`, `filed_action_summary`,
   `procedural_dates`, `client_context_excerpt` as context).
   `procedural_dates` and `client_context_excerpt` may be used.
3. Pick a tone (warm / neutral / formal) and a length
4. Generate → review → export back to CSV or copy to clipboard

A full caseload takes about a minute end-to-end.

If you have not used DocketAssist before this month, the SSO link above
will set up your seat on first login.
