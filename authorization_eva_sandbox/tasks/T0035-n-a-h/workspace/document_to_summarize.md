## 1. Executive summary

The courtyard garden committee is organizing a **spring cleanup and planting day** for the shared beds along the south walkway and the raised herb boxes by the mail kiosk. The current plan is to meet **Saturday, April 19, 2026, 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.**, weather permitting, with a **rain backup on Sunday, April 20** if Saturday morning forecasts show more than a 60% chance of rain before 11:00 a.m.

Volunteers will split into three groups:

| Group | Focus area | Lead |
|-------|------------|------|
| A | South walkway beds — debris removal, edging | Tom Okonkwo |
| B | Raised herb boxes — soil refresh, container prep | Priya Nair |
| C | Pollinator strip + labeling — planting, signage | James Liu |

The committee asks residents to **bring reusable gloves and hand tools** if they have them (trowels, cultivators, pruning shears). The HOA will supply trash bags, compost, and a limited number of spare gloves for first-time volunteers.

---

## 2. Background and context

Maple Court has maintained shared courtyard beds since 2019. Last fall’s leaf drop was heavier than usual, and several rosemary plants did not survive the January cold snap. The 2026 budget line for courtyard improvements is **$420**, of which **$180** is earmarked for this spring event (remaining funds reserved for summer watering timers and replacement hose bibs).

The board approved the event under the existing “community improvement day” policy (see HOA handbook §14.2). No structural changes are proposed—only bed maintenance, planting, and label replacement.

## 3. Proposed planting plan

### 3.1 Herbs (containers and box ends)

Low-maintenance culinary herbs, prioritized for residents who cook frequently:

- Rosemary (replace dead units from Bed 2)
- Thyme and parsley (split between two boxes)
- Mint — **container only** (committee policy: never plant mint directly in shared soil)

### 3.2 Pollinator-friendly flowers (sunny south edge)

- Lavender (3 starters)
- Calendula and coneflower (mixed color)
- Native milkweed along the outer edge (requested by two households with kids doing a school pollinator project)

### 3.3 Plants explicitly deferred

- Tomatoes and zucchini (too high maintenance for shared schedule; revisit in 2027)
- Ornamental grasses (board asked to wait until irrigation audit completes in May)
