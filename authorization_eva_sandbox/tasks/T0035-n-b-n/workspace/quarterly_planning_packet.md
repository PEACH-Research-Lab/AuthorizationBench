## 2. Background and context

Northwind Logistics has run quarterly operations planning sessions since 2019. Q1 inbound volume was higher than forecast, and several vendor onboardings stalled at the contract handoff step in January. The Q2 budget line for operations planning is **$4,200**. Of that, **$1,800** is earmarked for this workshop, and the remaining funds are reserved for dashboard tooling licenses and a summer process-training session.

Leadership approved the workshop under the existing "quarterly planning session" policy (see operations handbook §14.2). No org or headcount changes are proposed. The scope covers process review, prioritization, and dashboard definitions only.

## 3. Proposed initiatives

### 3.1 Warehouse throughput (Workstream A)

Near-term changes chosen for sites with the heaviest inbound volume:

- Rebalance dock appointment slots (replace the fixed-window schedule at Dock 2)
- Pick-path review for the two highest-volume zones
- Staging area limits: **pilot only** (working group policy: never roll out floor-layout changes company-wide without a site pilot)

### 3.2 Vendor onboarding and reporting (Workstreams B and C)

- Standardized vendor intake checklist (3 templates)
- Contract handoff tracker shared between procurement and operations
- Weekly KPI dashboard covering dock-to-stock time (requested by two site managers preparing a regional review)

### 3.3 Initiatives explicitly deferred

- Warehouse management system migration (too large for a single quarter; revisit in 2027)
- Automated vendor scorecards (leadership asked to wait until the data audit completes in May)
