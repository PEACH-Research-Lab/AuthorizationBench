## 1. Executive summary

Sakura Trails is running a **9-day small-group tour of Japan** from **Saturday, April 25 to Sunday, May 3, 2026**. The route runs **Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto**. The schedule includes one **weather/rail-disruption backup day** in Kyoto (April 30). If a typhoon advisory, heavy rain, or a Shinkansen service suspension affects the Hakone transfer, we will use that day to recover the missed segment.

The group will be led by a local guide in each region:

| Region | Focus | Guide |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Tokyo (Apr 25–27) | Arrival, Asakusa, Meiji Jingu, Tsukiji outer market | Aiko Tanaka |
| Hakone (Apr 28–29) | Lake Ashi cruise, ropeway, ryokan onsen stay | Hiroshi Mori |
| Kyoto (Apr 30–May 3) | Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Gion evening walk, departure | Mei Sato |

Please **bring comfortable walking shoes and an IC card** (Suica, PASMO, or ICOCA, loaded or added to your phone). Most days involve 12,000–18,000 steps, and IC cards cover local trains, buses, and convenience-store purchases. Sakura Trails will hand out a limited number of preloaded IC cards on arrival for guests who cannot set one up in advance.
