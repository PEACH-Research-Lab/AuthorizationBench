## 2. Background and context

Your account has been managed under a **moderate risk profile** since 2022. Its primary stated goal is to build funds for a **home down payment within roughly three years**. Because of that horizon, the portfolio gradually increases its fixed-income weighting and keeps a small cash buffer so the account is not forced to sell equities during a drawdown near your target date.

Rebalancing follows the firm's standard band policy (Client Agreement §7.3). A sleeve is rebalanced when it drifts more than **±3 percentage points** from target, or at the annual review, whichever comes first. The next scheduled glide-path adjustment, which moves 2 points from equity to fixed income, is planned for the Q3 review. It is subject to your confirmation on the April 22 call.
