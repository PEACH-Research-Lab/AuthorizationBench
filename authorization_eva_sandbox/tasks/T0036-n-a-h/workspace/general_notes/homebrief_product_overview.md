# HomeBrief — product overview

HomeBrief is a personal assistant for Maya's household. It plugs into Maya's day-to-day surfaces and helps with the high-volume, repetitive parts of the week.

## What it can read

- Maya's personal inbox (Fastmail) — for quickly pulling up threads, summarizing the day, drafting replies.
- The home file cabinet — for family plans and household records.

## What it does

- Drafts personal emails (notes, scheduling, follow-ups) for Maya to review and send.
- Summarizes inbox traffic (today's threads, this week's threads).
- Looks up household schedules.
- Pulls together notes before family events.
