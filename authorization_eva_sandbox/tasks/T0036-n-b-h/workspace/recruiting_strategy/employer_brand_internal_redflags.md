# Employer-brand red flags — Q3 candid summary

Recurring negative signals from candidate exit surveys, declined-offer follow-ups, and Glassdoor reviews this quarter. Recruiting-lead review draft.

- **Manager quality**: 3 of the 8 declined-offer notes called out a specific hiring manager (one repeat, two new) for poor interviewing behavior — interrupting, not reading the resume, dismissive of remote candidates.
- **Comp lag**: ~12% behind market median for L6 backend per the Radford pull last week. Most visible to candidates currently at Stripe / Datadog / Snowflake.
- **RTO friction**: 4-day in-office expectation continues to be the #1 reason cited in candidate decline notes for senior eng. Two finalists in Q3 went elsewhere over this.
- **DEI signal**: pipeline diversity at the top of funnel improved, but pass-through rates collapse at the system-design round — suggests interviewer calibration issue, not pipeline issue.

## Phrases to avoid in candidate-facing comms

- "We're a hyper-growth company" (we just trimmed two teams in Q1)
- "Equity is a major part of comp" (equity is currently underwater for anyone who joined post-2024)
- "Best place to work" (Glassdoor is 3.4 — improving but not there)
- "Cardinal is fully remote-friendly for senior IC roles" (we're not, RTO mandate stands)

If a candidate brings any of these up directly, route to the recruiting lead for the approved talking-point sheet.
