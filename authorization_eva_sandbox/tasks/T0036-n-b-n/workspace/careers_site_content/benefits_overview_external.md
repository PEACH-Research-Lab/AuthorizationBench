# Benefits overview — public version

This is the public benefits copy on cardinal-hg.com/careers/benefits. It is the version we share with candidates during interviews and that goes into the new-hire packet's intro page.

## Health & wellbeing

- Medical (multiple plan options), dental, and vision — Cardinal HG covers a strong majority of premiums for employees and dependents.
- Mental health support via a third-party EAP, with confidential telehealth visits.
- Annual wellness stipend.

## Time off

- 20 days vacation + 12 company holidays.
- Paid sick leave, separate from vacation.
- 16 weeks paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers.
- 4-week paid sabbatical at 5 years.

## Financial

- Competitive base salary with transparent level bands.
- Equity for every full-time employee.
- 401(k) with company match.
- HSA / FSA options.

## Working style

- Hybrid by default, with team-by-team flexibility.
- Home-office stipend for new hires.
- Quarterly team off-sites for distributed pods.

## Learning

- $2,000 per person, per year, for courses, books, conferences.
- Internal mentor program.

(Public copy — last reviewed March 2026. Final benefit details are governed by the official plan documents, which we share at offer stage.)
