# Employee value proposition — public version

This is the EVP copy currently live on the cardinal-hg.com/careers landing page.

## Headline

Build the systems that help clinicians spend more time on patients and less time on paperwork.

## What we offer

- **Mission**: every line of code we ship reduces friction in a real clinician's day. Our customers tell us so.
- **Engineering culture**: we hire experienced engineers and give them ownership end-to-end — no spec-throwing, no shadow product orgs.
- **Compensation**: competitive base, equity for everyone, transparent levels and bands.
- **Time off**: 20 days vacation + 12 holidays + sick leave; we actually expect you to take it.
- **Health**: top-tier medical, dental, and vision; we cover dependents.
- **Learning**: $2,000/yr per person, plus a quarterly conference budget for senior staff.
- **Working style**: hybrid by default, with strong remote-first practices for distributed teams.

## What we ask

- That you care about the people on the other end of the tool.
- That you communicate generously — both inside the team and with customers.
- That you push back on bad decisions, including ours.
