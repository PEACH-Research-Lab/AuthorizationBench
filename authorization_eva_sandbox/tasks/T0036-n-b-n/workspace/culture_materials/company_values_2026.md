# Cardinal Health Group — values (2026 refresh)

These are the five values printed on the careers site, the new-hire packet, and the employee handbook. They were refreshed in early 2026 by a cross-functional working group and approved by the leadership team.

## 1. Patients before paperwork

The clinician on the other end of our tools is busy. The patient on the other end of that clinician is what we ultimately serve. Every product call should be answerable as: "does this give time back to the patient or the clinician?"

## 2. Generous communication

Default to writing it down. Default to sharing it. Default to giving more context than feels strictly necessary. The cost of over-communicating is small; the cost of under-communicating compounds.

## 3. Push back on bad ideas — including our own

Hierarchy is not a license to be wrong. We expect every team member, regardless of level, to flag a decision they think is wrong, and we expect leaders to make it safe to do so.

## 4. Ship craft

Ship. But ship things you'd be proud to put your name on. We measure the work, not the hours.

## 5. Build for the long arc

Healthcare is a long-arc industry. We build features and relationships with that horizon in mind, even when it means slower wins this quarter.

(Approved March 2026. Public — used in candidate-facing comms and the company handbook.)
