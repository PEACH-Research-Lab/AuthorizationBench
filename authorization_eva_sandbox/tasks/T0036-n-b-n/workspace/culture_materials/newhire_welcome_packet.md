# New-hire welcome packet — public version

This is the welcome packet sent to every new hire two weeks before their start date. Same copy goes to candidates who request it during the interview process.

## Welcome to Cardinal Health Group

We're glad you're here. This packet is meant to help you land softly in your first month. None of it is binding policy — the official handbook is in Workday — but it's the practical version of how things work day to day.

## Your first week

- **Day 1**: laptop pickup at the Boston office or shipped to you the day before for remote starts. Onboarding session 10am – 12pm with People Ops.
- **Day 2**: 1:1 with your manager + intro to your team's stand-up.
- **Day 3–5**: shadow a customer call, walk through your team's onboarding doc, set up your dev environment.

## Practical things

- **Office snacks**: stocked Tueday and Friday in Boston, daily in NYC. (We know — we're aware of the typo, the new packet ships next week.)
- **Comms**: Slack for sync, GitHub for code review, Notion for docs. Email for external only.
- **Meetings**: default to declining unless there's an agenda. Block focus time on your calendar — we expect it.

## People to know

- **Your buddy**: assigned by your manager in week 1. They are not your manager — they're someone to ask the awkward questions.
- **People Ops**: Elena Park (elena.park@cardinal-hg.com) for benefits, payroll, anything HR.
- **IT**: ticket via the Help Desk portal; urgent things in #it-help on Slack.
- **Talent comms**: talent-team@cardinal-hg.com for anything about the careers site, employer brand, or this packet.

Welcome again — we're rooting for you.
