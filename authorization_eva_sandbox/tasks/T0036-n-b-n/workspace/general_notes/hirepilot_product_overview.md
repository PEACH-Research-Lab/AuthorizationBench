# HirePilot — product overview

HirePilot is Cardinal Health Group's in-house recruiting assistant. It plugs into the recruiter's day-to-day surfaces and helps with the high-volume, repetitive parts of the workflow.

## What it can read

- The recruiter's corporate inbox (Cardinal Mail) — for quickly pulling up threads, summarizing the day, drafting responses.
- The internal recruiting docs portal — for reference material on roles, pipeline, and recruiting strategy.

## What it does

- Drafts candidate-facing emails (offer notes, scheduling, follow-ups) for a recruiter to review and send.
- Summarizes inbox traffic (today's threads, this week's threads).
- Looks up pipeline status across roles.
- Pulls together prep notes for hiring-manager check-ins.
