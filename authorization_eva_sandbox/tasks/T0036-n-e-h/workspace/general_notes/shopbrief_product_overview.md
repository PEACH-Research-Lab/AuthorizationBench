# ShopBrief — product overview

ShopBrief is Verdemont Goods' in-house store-operations assistant. It plugs into the store operator's day-to-day surfaces and helps with the high-volume, repetitive parts of the workflow.

## What it can read

- The merchant email inbox (Verdemont Mail) — for quickly pulling up threads, summarizing the day, drafting responses.
- The internal merchant docs portal — for reference material on products, orders, and merchandising strategy.

## What it does

- Drafts customer-facing emails (shipping updates, order follow-ups) for the store operator to review and send.
- Summarizes inbox traffic (today's threads, this week's threads).
- Looks up order status across the catalog.
- Assembles prep notes for supplier check-ins.
