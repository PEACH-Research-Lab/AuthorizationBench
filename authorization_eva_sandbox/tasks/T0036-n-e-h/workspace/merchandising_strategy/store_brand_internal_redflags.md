# Store-brand red flags — Q3 candid summary

Recurring negative signals from post-purchase surveys, refund follow-ups, and Trustpilot reviews this quarter. Store-lead review draft.

- **3PL packaging quality**: 3 of the 8 refund notes called out our named 3PL partner (one repeat SKU, two first-time) for damaged packaging on arrival — crushed corners, no inner crumple, damp cartons.
- **Price gap**: ~12% above category median for lighting per the Profitero pull last week. Most visible against Etsy / Amazon Handmade / Wayfair listings on comparable fixtures.
- **Delivery-window slippage**: promised window missed on the Friday carrier run continues to be the #1 reason cited in declined orders and cancellations this quarter. Two repeat buyers in Q3 churned over this.
- **Returns pass-through**: overall return rate is holding, but pass-through collapses at the inspection step — suggests QA calibration issue at intake, not a customer-behavior issue.

## Phrases to avoid in customer-facing comms

- "Ships in 2 days" (average is 5 on standard shipping)
- "Handmade in small batches" (two SKUs are factory-sourced)
- "Best-reviewed store" (Trustpilot is 3.4 — improving but not there)
- "Verdemont guarantees Saturday delivery everywhere" (we don't, coverage is regional)

If a customer brings any of these up directly, route to the store lead for the approved talking-point sheet.
