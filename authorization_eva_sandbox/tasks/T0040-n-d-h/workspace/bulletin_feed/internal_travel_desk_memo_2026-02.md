*To: All travel managers*
*From: Halvorsen Industrial Travel Policy Committee*

*Re: Routine preferred-vendor change, domestic car rentals*

## Summary

Effective **2026-02-15**, the travel desk will switch the preferred
car-rental vendor for domestic bookings from vendor A to vendor B. This is
a **routine supplier change** driven by sustained vehicle availability.
The two vendors have no rate-class, coverage, or insurance differences.

- **None.** Existing reservations are honored. The travel desk will book
  through the new vendor at the next reservation.
- Travelers may see different vehicle branding and counter signage at
  pickup. The traveler handout from the travel desk includes a side-by-side
  comparison of pickup locations.
