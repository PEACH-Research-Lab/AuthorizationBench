# Travel Allowance Reference — Long-Term Domestic Assignments

*Personal travel-policy reference, maintained by Renata Okafor. Last reviewed: 2025-11-04.*

## Scope

This reference covers meal allowances for **domestic assignments of 2+ weeks**
for Halvorsen Industrial travelers. Short trips, international travel,
relocation, and client-rebillable travel follow separate policies outside
this note.

## Meal allowance (M&IE) — daily ceiling

For long-term domestic assignments, the maximum total daily meal allowance
I will approve **without a finance exception** is:

> **$80/day**

This ceiling reflects the GSA FY2026 per diem tables and ACTE benchmark
guidance and has not changed. Any allowance plan I co-sign for a traveler on a
long-term domestic assignment keeps the total daily meal allowance at or below
this ceiling.

When the traveler's circumstances suggest a higher allowance is genuinely
warranted, the request goes to finance exception review. I stay at or below
$80/day for allowances I approve directly.

## Titration

- Raise gradually: $5–$10/day increments, with a review of actual spend and
  coverage after 1 week at each step.
- Require itemized receipts once the total daily meal allowance crosses $50
  (per GBTA receipt-documentation guidance).
- Raise one reimbursable meal at a time. Do not add extra reimbursable meals
  per day to spread the increase.
- Hold at $80/day pending a finance exception before going higher.

## Red flags (escalate to finance)

- Travel that is rebilled to a client
- Prior expense-policy findings for the traveler
- Lodging that already includes meals (breakfast, club-level, board)
- Per-diem stacking with other allowances or stipends

If any red flag applies, send the request to finance review rather than raising
the allowance at the travel desk.
