# Internal Fulfillment Memo — Carrier Switch (Standard Ground Parcels)

*To: All support staff*
*From: Hearthline Home Goods Fulfillment & Returns Committee*
*Date: 2026-02-08*
*Re: Routine carrier change, standard-ground parcels*

## Summary

Effective **2026-02-15**, the fulfillment team will move standard-ground
parcel shipments from carrier A to carrier B. This is a **routine carrier
switch** made for capacity reasons. Delivery times, transit coverage, and
parcel insurance are the same under both carriers.

## Action required

- **None.** Existing orders in transit stay with their current carrier.
  New standard-ground shipments will go out with carrier B from the
  effective date.
- Customers may see a tracking number in a different format. The
  customer-facing FAQ from the fulfillment team includes a side-by-side
  example of both formats.

## Questions

Send questions to the fulfillment line at extension 4-7100 or to the chair
of the Fulfillment & Returns Committee below.
