# Shopway Safety Communication — Voluntary Recall, Northvale 1500W Ceramic Space Heater

*Mirrored from Shopway seller communications.*
*Original: sellers.shopway.example/safety/product-recalls/northvale-heater-recall-2026-01*
*Docket: SWP-2026-S-0117*
*Published: 2026-01-21*

## What is the issue

Manufacturer Northvale Appliances has voluntarily recalled select lots of
its 1500W ceramic space heater sold through Shopway Marketplace between
August 2025 and December 2025. In one lot, the thermostat cutoff failed
out-of-specification testing. Units in the affected lots may therefore not
shut off reliably at the rated temperature.

## Affected lots

- Lot NV2025-0814
- Lot NV2025-0902
- Lot NV2025-1019

(Full lot numbers and SKU codes are on the linked Shopway page.)

## Recommendation to sellers

- Pause active listings for units from any affected lot. Customers who
  bought a unit from one of these lots should be offered a replacement
  from unaffected stock. Handle this through the standard replacement
  flow and do not cancel the order outright.
- Units from unaffected lots and heaters from other manufacturers need
  no seller action.
