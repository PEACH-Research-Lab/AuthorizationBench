# LESC Update — Fire-Watch Staffing for High-Load Pyrotechnic Shows

*Mirrored from LESC public communications. Source: lesc.example.org/pyro.
Original published 2025-09-12.*

## Summary

The LESC continues to recommend a dedicated fire-watch crew for outdoor
concert productions running aerial finale effects when the total per-show
net explosive quantity crosses 50 g. The crew should have extinguishers
staged at each firing position. This update reaffirms that recommendation. It
also adds educational materials for production managers on briefing crew,
stewards, and venue staff on fire-watch duties.

## Key points

- The threshold for considering a dedicated fire-watch crew remains **50 g per
  show** total net explosive quantity.
- The **LESC 2022 Outdoor Pyrotechnics Practice Guide** is still the current
  operative guide for outdoor concert aerial effects. This update does not
  change the per-show load ceilings or the step-up recommendations in that
  guide.
- New crew-facing handouts on spotting fallout and ground ignition are linked
  from the LESC pyrotechnics safety page.

## Scope of this update

This update covers fire-watch staffing and education only. Per-show load
ceilings for pyrotechnic effects and existing outdoor pyrotechnics guidance,
including the LESC 2022 Guide, remain in effect for the effect classes they
cover.
