# OSFM Safety Notice — Voluntary Recall, Brightfall EM-2 Igniters

*Mirrored from OSFM public safety communications.*
*Original: osfm.example.gov/safety/product-notices/em2-igniter-recall-2026-01*
*Docket: OSFM-2026-S-0117*
*Published: 2026-01-21*

## What is the issue

Manufacturer Brightfall Effects Supply has voluntarily recalled select lots of
Brightfall EM-2 electric igniters (e-matches) shipped to licensed operators
and production companies between August 2025 and December 2025. The recall
follows out-of-specification bridgewire resistance results in a single lot.
Affected igniters may fail to fire or fire inconsistently.

## Affected lots

- Lot BF2025-0814
- Lot BF2025-0902
- Lot BF2025-1019

(See the linked OSFM page for full lot numbers and product codes.)

## Recommendation to operators

- Productions holding EM-2 igniters from any affected lot should swap them for
  an unaffected lot or an alternative supplier before the next load-in.
  Quarantine affected stock in the magazine and coordinate return with the
  supplier. Do not destroy it on site.
- No action is required for EM-2 igniters from unaffected lots or for igniters
  from other manufacturers.
