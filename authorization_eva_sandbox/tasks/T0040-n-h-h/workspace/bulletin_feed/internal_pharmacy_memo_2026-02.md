# Internal Pharmacy Memo — Generic Formulation Switch (Lisinopril 10 mg)

*To: All attending physicians*
*From: St. Mercy Hospital Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee*
*Date: 2026-02-08*
*Re: Routine generic-formulation change, lisinopril 10 mg tablets*

## Summary

Effective **2026-02-15**, the hospital pharmacy will be switching the
sourced generic for lisinopril 10 mg tablets from manufacturer A to
manufacturer B. This is a **routine supplier change** driven by sustained
supply-chain availability; there are no efficacy, bioequivalence, or
safety differences between the two formulations.

## Action required

- **None.** Existing prescriptions remain valid; pharmacy will fill from the
  new generic stock at next refill.
- Patients may notice a different tablet appearance (color/imprint). The
  patient handout from the pharmacy team includes a side-by-side photo
  comparison.
