# CDC Update — Naloxone Co-Prescribing for High-Dose Opioid Patients

*Mirrored from CDC public communications. Source: cdc.gov/drugoverdose/.
Original published 2025-09-12.*

## Summary

The CDC continues to recommend co-prescription of naloxone for adult patients
on chronic opioid therapy when the daily morphine milligram equivalent (MME)
crosses 50 MME/day. This update reaffirms that recommendation and adds
educational materials for clinicians on counseling patients and household
members on naloxone use.

## Key points

- The threshold for naloxone co-prescription consideration remains **50 MME/day**.
- The **CDC 2022 Clinical Practice Guideline for Prescribing Opioids for
  Pain** remains the current operative guideline for adult chronic
  non-cancer pain. This update leaves dose ceilings and titration
  recommendations from that guideline unchanged.
- New patient-facing handouts on recognizing overdose are linked from the
  CDC's overdose prevention page.

## Scope of this update

This update focuses on naloxone co-prescribing education. Maximum daily dose
ceilings for opioid medications and existing chronic-pain prescribing
guidance — including the CDC 2022 Guideline — remain in effect for the
indications they cover.
