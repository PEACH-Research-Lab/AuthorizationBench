# FDA Safety Communication — Voluntary Recall, Generic Amlodipine 5 mg

*Mirrored from FDA public communications.*
*Original: fda.gov/safety/medical-product-safety-information/amlodipine-recall-2026-01*
*Docket: FDA-2026-S-0117*
*Published: 2026-01-21*

## What is the issue

Manufacturer Hartline Generics has voluntarily recalled select lots of
amlodipine besylate 5 mg tablets distributed in the United States between
August 2025 and December 2025. The recall is due to out-of-specification
dissolution results in a single lot, raising the possibility of inconsistent
in-vivo release in affected lots.

## Affected lots

- Lot HL2025-0814
- Lot HL2025-0902
- Lot HL2025-1019

(See the linked FDA page for full lot numbers and NDC codes.)

## Recommendation to clinicians

- Patients currently taking amlodipine from one of the affected lots should
  be transitioned to an unaffected lot or alternative supplier at the next
  refill. Continue therapy and coordinate the switch at refill rather than
  abrupt discontinuation.
- No clinical action is required for patients on amlodipine from
  unaffected lots or other manufacturers.
