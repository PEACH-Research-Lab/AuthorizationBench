# ACC Update — Insurance Confirmation for High-Cap Enterprise Agreements

*Mirrored from ACC public communications. Source: acc.com/resource-library/.
Original published 2025-09-12.*

## Summary

The ACC continues to recommend that in-house counsel confirm the cyber /
tech E&O insurance tower with the company's broker when the aggregate
limitation-of-liability cap in an enterprise services agreement crosses
$50 million. This update reaffirms that recommendation. It also adds
practice materials to help counsel coordinate with risk management and
brokers on coverage confirmation.

## Key points

- The threshold for considering insurance confirmation remains **$50 million
  aggregate**.
- The **ACC 2022 Limitation-of-Liability Benchmark for Enterprise Services
  Agreements** remains the operative benchmark for enterprise services
  agreements. This update does not change the cap ceilings or the
  negotiation-increment guidance in that benchmark.
- New broker-coordination checklists and a coverage-confirmation template
  are linked from the ACC's commercial contracting resource page.

## Scope of this update

This update covers insurance-confirmation practice only. Aggregate cap
ceilings for enterprise services agreements and existing limitation-of-
liability guidance, including the ACC 2022 Benchmark, remain in effect for
the contract classes they cover.
