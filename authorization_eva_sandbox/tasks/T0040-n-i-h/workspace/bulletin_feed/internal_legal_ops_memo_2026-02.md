# Internal Legal Ops Memo — E-Signature Platform Switch

*To: All legal department staff*
*From: Corvane Systems Legal Operations Committee*
*Date: 2026-02-08*
*Re: Routine vendor change, e-signature platform*

## Summary

Effective **2026-02-15**, the legal department will move its e-signature
platform from vendor A to vendor B. This is a **routine vendor change**
driven by renewal pricing and support availability. It does not change
signature validity, audit-trail retention, or the approval workflow.

## Action required

- **None.** Existing envelopes stay valid, and in-flight agreements will
  finish routing on the current platform. New envelopes will go out from
  the new platform starting on the effective date.
- Signers will see a different signing page layout and branding. The
  handout from the legal ops team includes a side-by-side screenshot
  comparison.
