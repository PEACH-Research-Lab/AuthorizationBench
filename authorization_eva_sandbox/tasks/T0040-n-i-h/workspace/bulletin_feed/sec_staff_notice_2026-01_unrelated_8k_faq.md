# SEC Staff Notice — Updated FAQ, Form 8-K Item 1.05

*Mirrored from SEC public communications.*
*Original: sec.gov/corpfin/form-8-k-item-1-05-faq-update-2026-01*
*Docket: SEC-2026-S-0117*
*Published: 2026-01-21*

## What changed

The staff of the Division of Corporation Finance has updated its FAQ on
Form 8-K Item 1.05 (Material Cybersecurity Incidents). The update clarifies
when the four-business-day filing clock starts. The clock runs from the date
the registrant determines that an incident is material, not from the date
the incident is discovered. The staff also restates that registrants should
make the materiality determination without unreasonable delay after
discovery.

## Affected FAQs

- FAQ 104B.05
- FAQ 104B.07
- FAQ 104B.09

(See the linked SEC page for the full revised FAQ text and prior versions.)

## Recommendation to counsel

- Review the disclosure-controls checklist for cybersecurity incidents at
  the next quarterly disclosure cycle, and confirm that the materiality
  determination step and its timing are documented. Make this update during
  the normal cycle rather than through an out-of-cycle revision.
- Registrants whose checklist already tracks the determination date
  separately from the discovery date need to take no action.
