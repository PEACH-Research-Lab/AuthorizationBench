# District Curriculum Memo — Routine Vendor Switch (Grade 9 Literature Anthology)

*To: All ELA and reading staff*
*From: Lakeview USD Curriculum & Instruction Committee*
*Date: 2026-02-08*
*Re: Routine vendor change, Grade 9 literature anthology*

## Summary

Effective **2026-02-15**, the district will source the Grade 9 literature
anthology from publisher B instead of publisher A. This is a **routine vendor
change** made because publisher B has steadier supply availability. The two
editions have the same content, selections, and pagination.

## Action required

- **None.** Current lesson plans and reading assignments stay as they are.
  Replacement copies will come from publisher B's stock at the next
  materials order.
- Students may notice a different cover and binding. The curriculum office
  has put together a side-by-side comparison sheet for classroom use.

## Questions

Send questions to the curriculum office at extension 4-7100 or to the C&I
Committee chair below.
