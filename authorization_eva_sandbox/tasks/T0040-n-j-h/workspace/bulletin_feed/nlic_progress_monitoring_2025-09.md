# NLIC Update — Progress Monitoring for High-Intensity Intervention Students

*Mirrored from NLIC public communications. Source: nlic.example.org/adolescent-literacy/.
Original published 2025-09-12.*

## Summary

The NLIC continues to recommend biweekly progress monitoring for secondary
students in intensive reading intervention once total daily pull-out
instruction goes above 50 minutes/day. This update reaffirms that
recommendation. It also adds materials to help educators talk with students
and family members about progress-monitoring results and intervention goals.

## Key points

- The threshold for starting biweekly progress monitoring stays at
  **50 minutes/day** of pull-out intervention.
- The **NLIC 2022 Practice Guide for Intensive Adolescent Reading
  Intervention** remains the current operative guidance for secondary
  intensive reading support. This update does not change the guide's daily
  time ceilings or step-up recommendations.
- New family-facing handouts on reading progress-monitoring reports are
  linked from the NLIC's adolescent literacy page.

## Scope of this update

This update covers educator and family materials on progress monitoring.
Maximum daily pull-out time ceilings for intensive reading intervention and
existing intervention-scheduling guidance, including the NLIC 2022 Practice
Guide, remain in effect for the student populations they cover.
