# Reading Intervention Reference — Intensive (Tier 3) Adolescent Reading Support

*Personal instructional reference, maintained by Dana Whitfield. Last reviewed: 2025-11-04.*

## Scope

This reference covers pull-out reading intervention for **secondary students**
in my caseload. Summer programs, ELL newcomer programs, and special-education
services follow separate protocols outside this note.

## Tier 3 pull-out reading intervention (intensive small-group pull-out reading instruction) — daily ceiling

For intensive reading support for secondary students, the maximum total daily
pull-out time I will sign off on **without intervention-team review** is:

> **80 minutes/day**

This ceiling reflects the NLIC 2022 Practice Guide for Intensive Adolescent
Reading Intervention and the SLA intensive-intervention guidance and has not
changed. It exists so the student keeps core-class time. Any session plan I
co-sign for a Tier 3 student keeps total daily pull-out time at or below this
ceiling.

When instructional judgment suggests more time is genuinely needed, the
student goes to school intervention-team review; I stay at or below
80 minutes/day in my own caseload.

## Step-up

- Start low, go slow: 5–10 minutes/day increases at intervals of 2–3 weeks,
  checking progress data and engagement at each step.
- Begin biweekly progress monitoring once total daily pull-out exceeds
  50 minutes (per NLIC 2022 progress-monitoring guidance).
- Lengthen one existing session at a time — avoid adding extra session
  blocks across the school day.
- Hold at 80 minutes/day pending intervention-team review before going higher.

## Escalation flags (refer to intervention team)

- Student is already missing credit-bearing core classes
- Chronic attendance issues
- No response after two step-ups
- Scheduling conflicts with graduation requirements

When any flag is present, refer to the school intervention team rather than
increasing pull-out time on my own.
