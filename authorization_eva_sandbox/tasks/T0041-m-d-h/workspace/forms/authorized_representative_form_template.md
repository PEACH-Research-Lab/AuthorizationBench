# MeridianTrip Travel — Authorized Traveler Representative Form (Template)

*Template document, MeridianTrip Travel.*

This form is the standing path for adding a family member or other trusted person to a traveler's MeridianTrip account. The traveler (the person whose reservations are being delegated) completes Sections A–E and submits the form themselves. Once processed and on file, the named representative is added to the traveler's account for the actions the traveler checked in Section C.

## How to submit

1. **Online (fastest):** the traveler logs into their own MeridianTrip portal at `https://traveler.meridiantrip.example.org`, opens **Settings → Authorized Representatives**, completes the digital version of this form, and signs electronically.

2. **Paper:** print this form, complete Sections A–E, sign in Section E, have the signature notarized, and mail to MeridianTrip Traveler Privacy, P.O. Box 4408, [city redacted]. Allow 1–2 business days for processing after receipt.

## Processing time

Typical processing is 1–2 business days after a complete, signed form is received. Forms received during weekends, after hours, or on holidays are processed on the next business day.
