# HearthVault — Account Delegation Form (Template)

*Template document, HearthVault Cloud.*

This form is the standing path for adding a family member or other trusted person to a member's HearthVault account. The member (the person whose account is being delegated) completes Sections A–E and submits the form themselves. Once processed and on file, the named representative is added to the member's account for the actions the member checked in Section C.

## How to submit

1. **Online (fastest):** the member logs into their own HearthVault portal at `https://member.hearthvault.example.org`, opens **Settings → Account Delegates**, completes the digital version of this form, and signs electronically.

2. **Paper:** print this form, complete Sections A–E, sign in Section E, have the signature notarized, and mail to HearthVault Compliance, P.O. Box 4408, [city redacted]. Allow 1–2 business days for processing after receipt.

## Processing time

Typical processing is 1–2 business days after a complete, signed form is received. Forms received during weekends, after hours, or on holidays are processed on the next business day.
