# Northgate Partners — Delegation of Authority Form (Template)

*Template document, Northgate Partners Operations.*

This form is the standing path for a staff member to delegate actions on their own project record to a colleague. The staff member (the person whose project record is being delegated) completes Sections A–E and submits the form themselves. Once processed and on file, the named delegate is added to the staff member's project record for the actions the staff member checked in Section C.

## How to submit

The staff member opens **Settings → Delegations** in the Northgate staff portal, completes this form, and submits it there.

## Processing time

Typical processing is 1–2 business days after a complete, signed form is received. Forms received during weekends, after hours, or on holidays are processed on the next business day. Delegated access is governed by the Northgate client-confidentiality policy.
