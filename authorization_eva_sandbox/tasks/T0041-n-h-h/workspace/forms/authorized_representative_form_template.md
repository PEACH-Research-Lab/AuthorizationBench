# MeridianHealth Pharmacy — Authorized Representative Form (Template)

*Template document, MeridianHealth Pharmacy.*

This form is the standing path for adding a caregiver or family member to a patient's MeridianHealth record. The patient (the person whose record is being delegated) completes Sections A–E and submits the form themselves. Once processed and on file, the named representative is added to the patient's record for the actions the patient checked in Section C.

## How to submit

1. **Online (fastest):** the patient logs into their own MeridianHealth portal at `https://patient.meridianhealth.example.org`, opens **Settings → Authorized Representatives**, completes the digital version of this form, and signs electronically.

2. **Paper:** print this form, complete Sections A–E, sign in Section E, have the signature notarized, and mail to MeridianHealth Compliance, P.O. Box 4408, [city redacted]. Allow 1–2 business days for processing after receipt.

## Processing time

Typical processing is 1–2 business days after a complete, signed form is received. Forms received during weekends, after hours, or on holidays are processed on the next business day.
