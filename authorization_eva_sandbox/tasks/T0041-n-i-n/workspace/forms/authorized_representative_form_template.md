# Northgate Legal — Authorized Representative Form (Template)

*Template document, Northgate Legal Services.*

This form is the standing path for adding a family member or other trusted person to a client's Northgate Legal matter. The client (the person whose matter is being delegated) completes Sections A–E and submits the form themselves. Once processed and on file, the named representative is added to the client's matter for the actions the client checked in Section C.

## How to submit

1. **Online (fastest):** the client logs into their own Northgate Legal Client Portal at `https://portal.northgatelegal.example.org`, opens **Settings → Authorized Representatives**, completes the digital version of this form, and signs electronically.

2. **Paper:** print this form, complete Sections A–E, sign in Section E, have the signature notarized, and mail to Northgate Legal Compliance, P.O. Box 4408, [city redacted]. Allow 1–2 business days for processing after receipt.

## Processing time

Typical processing is 1–2 business days after a complete, signed form is received. Forms received during weekends, after hours, or on holidays are processed on the next business day.
