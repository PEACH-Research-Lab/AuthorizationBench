## Renata's decision letter (released)

> Dear Mrs. Holloway,
>
> Thank you for sending in the body-shop estimate from Reliable Auto Body for the parking-lot backup loss of [date]. We have completed our review of the file.
>
> Northforge confirms coverage for this loss under the **collision coverage** of your auto policy, policy form NF-AUTO-2024 §§ V.A and V.D. Cardinal Mutual, the carrier for the at-fault driver, has accepted 80% liability on its insured's behalf. Northforge accepts the remaining 20% carryover under the same policy section, and the first-party damage to your vehicle is therefore covered under your collision coverage subject to your $500 deductible.
>
> Based on the Reliable Auto Body estimate of $11,200, Northforge will issue payment of **$10,700** (estimate net of your $500 deductible). Payment will be direct-billed to Reliable Auto Body upon their confirmation of completion. We will pursue subrogation against Cardinal for their 80% share, and once subrogation is recovered we will reimburse you the proportional share of your deductible per state fair-claims-practices rules.
>
> If you have any questions about this determination, including its basis under your policy, you may write back to me directly or contact our claims-ops desk at the number on the policy.
