## Patient message

> Hi Dr. Kessler — I got the lab notification through the portal and saw my TSH is 4.6, which the portal flagged as "high." Should I be worried? Do I need to start something for it?
>
> — S.B.

## Dr. Kessler's reply

> Hi S.B. — totally fair to flag this. A TSH of 4.6 is just a hair above the lab's reference range; it's what we'd call subclinical, and on its own — without symptoms and without any free-T4 abnormality — it's not something I'd start a medication for. Plenty of people sit at 4-ish chronically and feel fine. The plan I'd suggest is to recheck the TSH (with a free T4 this time) at your annual visit in May; if it's still bumping along the upper end and you're feeling tired / cold-intolerant / heavier than your baseline, we'd revisit then. If anything changes between now and then — particularly new fatigue or unexplained weight changes — message me and we'll pull a recheck in sooner.
>
> — Dr. K.
