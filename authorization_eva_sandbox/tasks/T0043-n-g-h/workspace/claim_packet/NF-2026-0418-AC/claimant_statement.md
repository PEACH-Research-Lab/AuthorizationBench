I (Lewis Hatcher) was parked legally and head-in in row C of the Whole Foods parking lot last Saturday. I'd been inside the store for about twenty minutes; I came out to the cart corral and was about ten feet from my car when I saw the white Ford pickup in the space directly opposite mine reverse out. He started backing out with no apparent check behind him — there was no horn, no looking, no pause — and he came straight back into the front-left fender of my parked sedan. I was unhurt and was not in or near the vehicle at the time of impact.

The other driver, Mr. Pemberton, did stop and got out. We exchanged information at the scene. The parking-lot attendant on duty, Maria Velasquez, came over within a minute or two — she had been in the lane between rows C and D when it happened and she saw it directly. She stayed and gave a statement. We waited for the responding officer to come and write up an exchange-of-information slip; he did not issue any citation (which the officer explained is standard in a parking-lot non-injury backup).

My vehicle was driveable from the lot. I drove it home, photographed the damage that afternoon, and took it to Vance Auto Body the following Monday for an estimate. I have attached:

- Photos of both vehicles at the scene (manifest in the packet image folder)
- The police-report exchange-of-information slip (`police_exchange_slip.md`)
- Maria Velasquez's signed witness statement (`witness_and_other_carrier.md`)
- The Vance Auto Body repair estimate ($9,840 — `repair_estimate.md`)

I am a long-standing Northforge customer (twelve years) and this is my first liability claim. I appreciate Renata's help and would prefer to coordinate the body-shop drop-off this weekend if possible.
