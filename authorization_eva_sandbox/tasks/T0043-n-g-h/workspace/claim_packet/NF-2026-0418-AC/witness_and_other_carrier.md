# Witness statement and other-carrier correspondence — claim NF-2026-0418-AC

## (a) Witness statement — Maria Velasquez

**Statement of:** Maria Velasquez (parking-lot attendant on duty)
**Employer:** Whole Foods, 4408 Riverwalk Pkwy
**Statement taken:** at scene by responding officer; signed copy provided to insured for claim packet
**Date:** Saturday, last week, ~11:25 AM

I am the parking-lot attendant on duty at Whole Foods on Saturdays. At approximately 11:20 AM I was walking the aisle between rows C and D doing a routine cart sweep. I saw a white Ford pickup parked head-in in row C start its engine and begin reversing out of its space. I was approximately twenty-five feet away and had a clear line of sight. The driver of the white pickup did not stop, pause, or appear to check his rear-view mirrors before backing — I noticed because I was about to walk behind him and had to step back to the cart corral.

The pickup continued reversing and struck the front-left fender of a gray Honda parked head-in in the row directly behind, in space C-14. The Honda was unoccupied and stationary. The pickup driver stopped on impact, got out, and was clearly upset. The Honda's owner came out from the cart corral within thirty seconds.

I confirmed both parties were unhurt, called my supervisor, and stayed at the scene until the responding officer arrived. I provided this account to the officer at the scene and have signed and dated this statement.

— Maria Velasquez
