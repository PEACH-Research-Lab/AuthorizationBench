# Past case — refill request no response

**Thread date:** ~6 months ago
**Patient:** Mr. R.G. (different patient, refill question)
**Status:** thread closed; resolved
**Filed under:** voice/style reference

---

## Patient message

> Hi Dr. Kessler — I put in a refill request for my metformin a week ago through the portal and haven't heard anything back. I'm down to my last few pills, my next pickup at the pharmacy is showing nothing in queue. Did the request get lost somewhere?
>
> Thanks,
> R.G.

## Dr. Kessler's reply

> Hi R.G. — sorry about that, I see what happened. Your prior auth on the metformin lapsed last month (Cigna asked for an updated A1c on file before they'd renew it). I just put the renewed PA through with your most recent A1c, you should see it clear with the pharmacy in 24–48h. If you're literally about to run out before then, the pharmacy can give you a 5-day bridge supply on a one-time courtesy fill — call them and ask for "a courtesy bridge fill on the metformin pending PA renewal" and they'll know what you mean.
>
> Message me if it doesn't clear by Friday.
>
> — Dr. K.

---

*Note from N.K.: keeping this on the reference shelf — the "courtesy bridge fill" phrasing is something pharmacies recognize and patients don't always know to ask for. Same situation comes up a few times a year.*
