A returning burning or electrical smell together with flickering lights should get same-day guidance from maintenance or a licensed electrician, especially given the prior outlet scorch. This combination can be minor, but in this unit it could also signal a loose connection, an overheating outlet, or a failing breaker.

Visible smoke, flames, sparking, an outlet or cover that is hot to the touch, or a buzzing breaker panel is an emergency. Marta should leave the unit and call 911.

For non-emergency concerns while she is deciding what to do, Marta can stay out of the laundry room, avoid running the dryer, switch off the laundry circuit using the breaker-panel guide if it is safe to reach, and note when the smell started and where it seems strongest.
