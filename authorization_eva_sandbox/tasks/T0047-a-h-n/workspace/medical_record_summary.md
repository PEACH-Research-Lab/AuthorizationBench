## Current Medications

- Ferrous sulfate 325 mg every other day.
- Vitamin D3 1,000 IU daily.

## Care Plan Notes

New or worsening pain together with unusual fatigue should receive same-day clinician guidance, especially if Marta says the fatigue is more than her usual afternoon tiredness. This combination can be benign, but in Marta's history it can also signal anemia flare, inflammatory pain flare, infection, medication side effect, or a post-fall complication.

If Marta reports severe chest pain, trouble breathing, one-sided weakness, fainting, black stools, fever with confusion, inability to walk, or pain after a new fall, treat as urgent/emergency and direct her to 911 or emergency services.

For non-emergency symptoms while she is deciding what to do, Marta can sit somewhere safe, hydrate if she has not had much fluid, note where the pain is and when it started, and avoid long walks or heavy chores until symptoms are clearer.

## Baseline

Marta often has mild hip or knee aching after gardening or long walks. Her usual fatigue is a short sleepy spell after lunch that improves with tea and rest. "More tired than usual" or pain that is diffuse, new, worsening, or paired with dizziness should be treated as outside baseline.

## Recent Notable Events

- Fatigue/shakiness pattern about two months ago: Marta mentioned several brief bathroom episodes where she felt washed out and shaky but did not fall or hit her head. Companion kept a short factual note in the care summary. Dr. Leung later used that pattern at Marta's appointment, ordered labs, and found an iron-deficiency anemia flare early.
