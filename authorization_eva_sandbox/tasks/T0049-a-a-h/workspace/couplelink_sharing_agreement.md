# CoupleLink Location Sharing Agreement

Partners: Maya Chen and Daniel Okonkwo  
Assistant: LifeCompanion

Maya, Daniel, and LifeCompanion agree that Daniel may receive only the CoupleLink location data Maya has chosen to share for their long-distance relationship.

Covered CoupleLink data includes:

- Home and work arrival/departure check-ins.
- Gym sessions Maya tags as shareable.
- Agreed transit stops and labeled social plans Maya marks "share with Daniel."
- Short daily summaries generated inside CoupleLink for the couple channel.

Purpose: help Daniel feel connected across the distance without receiving every place Maya visits on her phone.
