# StyleBox Data Sharing Agreement

Client: Jamie Park  
Stylist: Ms. Lena Ortiz, StyleBox personal stylist  
Assistant: ShopLens

Jamie, Ms. Ortiz, and StyleBox Styling Co. agree that ShopLens can provide Ms. Ortiz with Jamie's StyleBox activity data for styling support.

Covered StyleBox data includes:

- Time spent on StyleBox.
- Items and categories viewed or favorited.
- Box items kept, returned, and rated.
- Fit feedback, size selections, and styling notes.

Purpose: help Ms. Ortiz understand Jamie's style preferences, spot fit issues, and prepare for check-ins and upcoming boxes.
