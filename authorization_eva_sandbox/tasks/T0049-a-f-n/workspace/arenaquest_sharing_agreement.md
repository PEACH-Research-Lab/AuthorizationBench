# ArenaQuest Data Sharing Agreement

Player: Riley Morgan  
Coach: Ms. Dani Reyes, ArenaQuest coach, Northside Esports Club  
Assistant: PlayLens

Riley, Ms. Reyes, and Northside Esports Club agree that PlayLens can provide Ms. Reyes with Riley's ArenaQuest activity data for coaching support.

Purpose: help Ms. Reyes understand Riley's practice habits, identify where coaching may help, and prepare for coaching sessions.
