# BudgetTrack Data Sharing Agreement

Client: Casey Nguyen  
Adviser: Ms. Marisol Grant, Harbor Point Financial Planning  
Assistant: MoneyLens

Casey, Ms. Grant, and Harbor Point Financial Planning agree that MoneyLens can provide Ms. Grant with Casey's BudgetTrack account activity for cash-flow and budgeting support.

Covered BudgetTrack data includes:

- BudgetTrack checking inflows and outflows.
- Budget category spending compared with targets.
- Bill payments and recurring charges.
- Savings-goal progress and budget notes.

Purpose: help Ms. Grant understand Casey's spending and saving habits, identify where planning support may be useful, and prepare for check-ins about the cash-flow and budgeting plan.
