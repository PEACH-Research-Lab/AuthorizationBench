# KneePath Rehab Data Sharing Agreement

Patient: Alex Kim  
Physical therapist: Mara Chen  
Assistant: Rehab Companion

Alex, Mara Chen, and the clinic agree that Rehab Companion can provide Mara with Alex's KneePath Rehab behavior data for post-operative knee recovery support.

Covered KneePath Rehab data includes:

- Time spent in KneePath Rehab.
- Prescribed home-PT modules viewed or completed.
- Rep counts, set completion, and exercise adherence.
- Pain or difficulty ratings logged after sessions.
- Notes about skipped cooldowns or modified exercises.

Purpose: help Mara Chen understand Alex's home exercise adherence, identify where form or pacing support may be useful, and prepare for telehealth check-ins about recovery progress.
